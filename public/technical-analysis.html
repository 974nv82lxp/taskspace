How the analysis works

  • Combined signal: available RSI, moving-average and MACD rules contribute bullish or bearish points. Missing indicators are excluded.
  • Trend: compares recent price action and available moving averages. Mixed or insufficient evidence is shown as sideways/unclear.
  • Support and resistance: uses local swing lows and highs from the available historical candles.
  • RSI alerts: RSI at or above 70 is often described as overbought; at or below 30 is often described as oversold. Neither guarantees a reversal.
  • MACD: the relationship between the MACD line and its signal line helps describe momentum.

This page provides informational technical analysis, not investment advice. Signals are based on historical data, can lag or conflict, and do not guarantee future performance or profitable trades. Always consider risk, costs and other information.
